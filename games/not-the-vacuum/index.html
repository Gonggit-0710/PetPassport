Not the Vacuum!

Sniff out hidden treats, one tile at a time. Each number tells you how many vacuums are hiding in the tiles around it. Use the clues to mark vacuums with a paw, and open every safe tile to win.

  • Three levels: Rookie, Skilled, Pro
  • Fill your treat jar and collect 24 pups in your Pup Album
  • Five new missions every day, including hidden ones
  • No login needed.
